5. Network requests

The app uses the internet for three things:

  1. Past draw display. The board shows recent official Powerball draws by reading a public dataset from New York State Open Data (Socrata). The app only reads that list. It does not send your saved numbers, settings, or identity. This request goes to infrastructure in the United States.
  2. Ads. Google AdMob loads banner and rewarded video ads (see section 6).
  3. App updates. If a newer version is on Google Play, the app may check Play and start an in-app update. That check goes to Google Play, not to our own server.
Like any internet request, your device’s IP address is visible to the server that answers. Those operators handle it under their own policies. We do not log it. We do not use Firebase.

5. 네트워크 통신

앱은 다음 세 가지를 위해 인터넷을 사용합니다.

  1. 과거 추첨 표시. 게임 보드는 뉴욕주 Open Data(Socrata)의 공개 데이터셋에서 최근 공식 파워볼 추첨 결과를 읽어오기만 합니다. 저장한 번호, 설정, 신원을 보내지 않습니다. 이 요청은 미국 쪽 인프라로 갑니다.
  2. 광고. Google AdMob이 배너 광고와 보상형 동영상 광고를 불러옵니다(제6항).
  3. 앱 업데이트. Google Play에 새 버전이 있으면 앱이 Play에 확인한 뒤 인앱 업데이트를 시작할 수 있습니다. 이 확인은 우리 서버가 아니라 Google Play로 갑니다.
인터넷 통신의 성격상 요청을 받는 서버에는 이용자의 IP 주소가 전달됩니다. 해당 사업자의 정책에 따라 처리되며, 개발자는 이를 로그로 남기지 않습니다. 이 앱은 Firebase를 사용하지 않습니다.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free. We use Google AdMob banner ads and rewarded video ads so we can keep it free.

What Google may collect

Google, not the developer, may process information such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Approximate region (inferred from IP)
  • Device information (model, OS version, and similar)
  • Ad impressions, clicks, and whether a rewarded ad was completed

We do not receive a copy of that data in a form that identifies you. Details:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services

Consent in the European Economic Area and UK

Where required by law, the app shows Google’s User Messaging Platform consent form before personalized ads. When that form is required for your region, an Ads & privacy control is shown in the app menu so you can open privacy options again.

How to limit ad personalization

On Android you can usually go to Settings → Google → Ads, then turn on Opt out of Ads Personalization or delete the advertising ID. Menu names vary by device. Ads can still appear; they may be less personalized.

Tarot & Saju: Lotto Redball (the “App”) complies with applicable laws, including US privacy laws and the Korean Personal Information Protection Act, and handles information as described below. The App has no sign-up, no login, and no server storage. Saved numbers and readings stay on your device only, and the App uses Google AdMob to show ads. The App is for entertainment only and is not affiliated with Powerball, the Multi-State Lottery Association, or any official lottery.

1. Information We Handle

1-1. Information the operator does not collect

The App does not ask for your name, email, phone number, address, or password. There is no member database and no operator server.

1-2. Information processed only on your device

The following is processed and stored only on your device and is never sent to the operator.

InformationPurposeStorage
Date of birth and gender entered for a Saju reading Calculating Saju, five elements, and white-ball/red-ball numbers Used only during calculation and not stored, including in saved records

The Four Pillars summary is derived from your date of birth, so please manage saved records with care if you share your device.

1-3. Information Google AdMob may collect

The App uses Google AdMob (Google LLC) for a top banner ad and rewarded ads. When ads are shown, Google may automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID (AAID or similar device ad identifier)
  • IP address, device type, OS version, app version
  • Ad requests, impressions, clicks, and rewarded-ad completion
  • Approximate region (based on IP)
  • Technical data needed for fraud prevention and ad measurement

This is processed for ad delivery, personalized/non-personalized ads, invalid-traffic prevention, and ad revenue settlement under the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising policies.

2. Purposes of Use

  1. Providing entertainment number suggestions based on tarot and Saju
  2. Showing your saved numbers again on the same device
  3. Daily use limits and rewarded-ad unlocks
  4. Showing ads to cover operating costs
  5. Handling errors and abuse (within what the ad SDK processes)

We do not use information for marketing emails, calls, or external member lists.

3. Network Use and Permissions

Numbers are generated on your device only from tarot cards and Saju elements. The App does not contact any operator server or look up winning numbers. The internet is used only for ads and Google Play features.

PermissionWhy it is used
Internet (INTERNET)Loading ads
Advertising ID (AD_ID)Showing Google AdMob ads (added by Android when AdMob is used)

The App does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage permissions.

4. Retention

  • Saved records: Until you delete them in the App or uninstall it. The operator cannot view or keep them remotely.
  • Date of birth and gender: Not kept after calculation.
  • Daily counts: Reset on your device when the date changes.
  • Ad data: As set out in Google AdMob's policies.

5. Third Parties and Service Providers

The App does not sell or provide your information to third parties. When ads run, technical data needed for ad requests is sent to Google AdMob, which processes it to provide the ad service.

RecipientItemsPurposeRetention

We do not use cloud member databases, analytics tools, or push servers. If that changes, we will update this policy. Requests from authorities follow applicable law.

7. Deletion

  • On-device data is removed when you uninstall the App, reset the device, or use the in-app delete function.
  • The operator keeps no user records on a server, so there is nothing to delete server-side.
  • Ad provider data is deleted under that provider's policies.

8. Your Rights

  • You can view and delete saved numbers in the App's saved list.
  • Uninstalling the App removes saved records and usage counts on that device.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit personalized ads, in Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Privacy inquiries: contact@wsub.app

Because the App has no accounts, there is no server-side data to access, correct, or delete. For advertising ID requests, we will also point you to Google's tools.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in Korea) and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Real lottery tickets may be purchased only by people who meet the minimum age set by their state or country (18 or older in most US states). Number suggestions in the App are for entertainment only.

10. Security

  • The App does not collect sign-up information or passwords.
  • Saved records stay on your device and are not synced to other devices.
  • Ad communication uses HTTPS.

Records on your device may be exposed if you share the device or use a rooted device.

11. About Ads

  • The App is free and contains ads.
  • It shows a top banner ad, and after 2 free uses per day (tarot reveals and Saju readings combined) a rewarded ad that unlocks unlimited use for the day.
  • Ad content is provided by Google and advertisers; the operator does not control all ad content.
  • Watching ads has no effect on the odds of any number winning.

12. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will notify you in the App or on this page and update the effective date.

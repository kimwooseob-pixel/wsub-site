Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Wsub ("we", "us") handles information in the Android app Dance With Me: Groove Together (package com.wsub.dancewithme, the "App").

1. Summary

  • The App has no sign-up and no user accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location.
  • Your dance settings, poses, favorites, and any music files you add stay on your device. We do not upload your own music files.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob and streams music through Google Firebase. These services may collect limited device data as described below.

2. Information stored only on your device

  • App settings (theme, art style, background, dancer looks, pose adjustments, imported dance moves).
  • Music files you add with "My MP3", and downloaded songs cached for offline playback.
  • Favorites, playback preferences, and images you export (PNG/SVG), which are saved to your device.

This data is kept in the App's local storage and is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

3. Information processed by third-party services

ServicePurposeData that may be processed
Google Firebase (Anonymous Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage)Loading the song list and streaming musicAn anonymous identifier created by Firebase, IP address, basic device and request information
Google AdMobShowing banner and rewarded adsAdvertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interaction data

These services are operated by Google and handle data under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy · Firebase Privacy · How AdMob uses data.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. International transfer

The services in section 3 are operated by Google LLC on servers outside your country, including in the United States, so the data listed there is transferred abroad during processing.

RecipientGoogle LLC (Firebase · AdMob)
CountriesThe United States and other countries where Google operates data centers
Items transferredThe items listed in section 3
PurposeLoading the song list, streaming music, serving and measuring ads
RetentionPer Google's own data retention policies

Wsub itself transfers no user information abroad. If you prefer not to have personalised ads, use the device settings described in section 3.

5. Microphone, camera, and location

The App does not use the microphone, camera, or your location. The dancers react to the music played inside the App, which is analyzed on your device only.

6. How we use information

Information is used only to provide music playback, show ads that support the free App, and keep the App working reliably. We do not sell personal information.

7. Data retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you clear it or uninstall the App. Data held by Google services is retained according to Google's policies. For any request about your data, contact us at the email below.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

Network communication with Firebase and AdMob uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact

이 정보는 앱의 로컬 저장소에만 보관되며 개발자에게 전송되지 않습니다. 앱 데이터를 지우거나 앱을 삭제하면 언제든 삭제됩니다.
